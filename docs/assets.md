# Third-party assets

One line per binary asset that is not Iridesium's own: what it is, where it
came from, who made it, under which licence, and where it lives in this
repository. Nothing yet: the textures are drawn by `tools/make_textures.py`,
and `sounds/ping.wav` (the Fork's chime) came with the engine's template and
is the engine's, made for it.
